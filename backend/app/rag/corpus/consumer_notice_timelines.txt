Title: Legal Notice Response Timelines and Consumer Practice
Source: Bar Council of India Civil Practice Guide & Legal Services Authorities Advisory
Source URL: https://nalsa.gov.in/acts-rules
Classification: General Reference, Not Verified Statutory Code

Summary & Standard Rules:
1. When a formal legal notice or demand notice is served, the sender conventionally provides a response window between fifteen (15) to thirty (30) business days from the date of receipt.
2. A response window demanding compliance in under seven (7) days is unusually tight and typically intended to cause undue duress, unless related to specific emergency injunctive relief.
3. Recipients are advised to acknowledge receipt in writing within the stipulated window, dispute unjustified claims explicitly, and preserve all delivery receipts and postal tracking records.
4. Silence or failure to reply to a formal legal notice can be argued as implied admission or lack of defense in subsequent civil disputes.
