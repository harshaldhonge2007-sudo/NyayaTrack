Title: Standard Freelance Contract Termination, Non-Compete Scope, and Payment Timelines in India
Source: Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Section 27) & Consumer Affairs Advisory
Source URL: https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/acts-and-rules/consumer-protection
Classification: General Reference, Not Verified Statutory Code

Summary & Standard Rules:
1. Standard independent contractor and freelance consulting agreements typically specify between fifteen (15) to thirty (30) calendar days written notice for no-fault termination.
2. Invoices are conventionally payable within 15 to 30 calendar days (Net-15 or Net-30) following submission of approved deliverables. Immediate withholding without formal dispute notice is anomalous.
3. Under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act 1872, any agreement that restrains anyone from exercising a lawful profession, trade, or business of any kind is void to that extent. Post-termination non-compete clauses against individual gig workers or freelancers are broadly unenforceable in Indian courts.
4. Clauses imposing unlimited indemnification or unlimited liability onto an individual gig worker or freelancer deviate significantly from fair standard practice.
