Title: Fairness in Unilateral Contract Modifications and Termination Rights
Source: Indian Law Commission 103rd Report on Unfair Terms in Contracts & Supreme Court Precedents (Pioneer Urban v. Govindan Raghavan)
Source URL: https://lawcommissionofindia.nic.in/reports/report103.pdf
Classification: General Reference, Not Verified Statutory Code

Summary & Standard Rules:
1. Terms of a contract that grant one party sole and absolute discretion to alter financial amounts, unilaterally terminate without cause, or impose penalties without reciprocal rights for the counter-party are termed unilateral or unconscionable.
2. Standard contracting norms require reasonable advance notification (usually 30 days) and mutual consent before material revisions to terms such as rent, scope of work, fees, or notice periods take effect.
3. If an amended contract gives one party unilateral power to evict or terminate on 7 days notice while binding the other party to 30 or 60 days, this represents a severe procedural and substantive imbalance.
