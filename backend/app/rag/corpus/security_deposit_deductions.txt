Title: Guidelines on Rental Security Deposits and Lawful Deductions in India
Source: Model Tenancy Act, Chapter IV (Deposit & Rent Guidelines)
Source URL: https://mohua.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Model_Tenancy_Act_English.pdf
Classification: General Reference, Not Verified Statutory Code

Summary & Standard Rules:
1. The Model Tenancy Act limits residential tenancy security deposits to a maximum of two (2) months' rent.
2. Security deposits must be returned to the tenant at the time of vacating and handing over vacant physical possession of the premises, or within a maximum of thirty (30) days.
3. Landlords are only permitted to deduct lawful arrears of rent, utility bills, or actual damages resulting from tenant negligence. Deductions for normal fair wear and tear (such as ordinary painting deterioration, natural sun fade, or aged fixtures) are considered unfair and non-standard.
4. Any clause permitting forfeiture of the entire deposit for minor disputes without itemized expenditure receipts is high risk.
