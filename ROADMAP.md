# NyayaTrack — Product Roadmap & Future Architecture

### Post-Hackathon Extensions

#### Phase 1: Small Business & MSME Compliance Engine
- **GST & Labour Notice Processing**: Automated intake for Section 73/74 GST show-cause notices, ESIC inspections, and PF compliance demands.
- **Vendor Contract Repository**: Multi-vendor timeline tracking invoice payment cycles against the MSMED Act 45-day statutory payment mandate.

#### Phase 2: Bar Council Verified Advocate Marketplace
- **Direct Brief Forwarding**: One-click dispatch of the generated "Questions for a Lawyer" and structured clause diff directly into an advocate's CRM.
- **Micro-Consultations**: 15-minute fixed-fee video/phone consults with verified advocates in the client's city or judicial district.
- **Escrow Settlement**: UPI escrow integration where fees are held until consultation notes are submitted.

#### Phase 3: Statutory Expansion & Multi-State Tenancy Acts
- Expansion beyond the Model Tenancy Act to state-specific tenancy legislation:
  - Karnataka Rent Control Act
  - Maharashtra Rent Control Act
  - Delhi Rent Act
  - Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act
- Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC/DCDRC) notice standards.

#### Phase 4: Security & Compliance
- **DPDPA 2023 Compliance**: End-to-end encryption with client-side zero-knowledge decryption.
- **Automated PII Redaction**: Client-side scrubbing of Indian identifiers (Aadhaar numbers, PAN cards, bank account details) prior to model ingestion.
- **Digital Signature Audit**: eSign / Aadhaar XML signature validation to confirm authenticity of incoming notices.
